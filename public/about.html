We are
Hive Society

We are the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign's long form improv team. Each year we add a whole new team to our society and build them up to be well rounded improv-ers.

Hive Society was reborn in the 22-23 academic year and continues to grow to greater and greater heights. If you want to get in on the action and audition, sign up for our mailing list and keep an eye out for audition opportunities.
